Research

SATARK’s open-source rebuild is grounded in security research themes: threat analysis, ranking, explainability, and multi-domain detection. Publication metadata below is a placeholder until verified references are added.

Original SATARK research

Foundational ideas around automated analysis and ranking of known threats. Detailed paper titles, venues, and years are pending verification.

See also Research origins in the documentation.

Insider threat research

Behavioral analytics for unusual endpoint activity (for example USB and file access patterns). The repository includes an insider plugin and a legacy LSTM demo retained for research comparison.

Threat scoring

Transparent risk scoring with contributing factors, evidence, confidence, reasoning, and knowledge references — not unexplained numeric scores.

Explainable security analytics

Detections should answer “why was this classified as malicious?” AI may assist with summaries, but detections must remain reproducible without an LLM.
