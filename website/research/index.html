Research

SATARK’s open-source rebuild is grounded in peer-reviewed and conference research on insider threat prediction, behavioral analysis, malware techniques, and web security. Publication details below are taken from confirmed BibTeX records.

Original SATARK research

Early architecture and incident-response work established detection of insider cyber threats as a primary research direction (2012 onward), later formalized in behavioral prediction studies and doctoral research.

See also Research origins in the documentation.

Insider threat research

Behavioral analysis for predicting and detecting insider threats — including deep learning approaches and Windows forensic analysis — forms the core of the SATARK research lineage.

Threat scoring

Transparent risk scoring with contributing factors, evidence, confidence, reasoning, and knowledge references — not unexplained numeric scores.

Explainable security analytics

Detections should answer “why was this classified as malicious?” AI may assist with summaries, but detections must remain reproducible without an LLM.
